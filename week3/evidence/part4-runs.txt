as the lattice heats: 3397.2 spins, 82.9% of L^2 = 4096, at T = 2.0, falling
to 35.7 spins, 0.87%, at T = 2.6. L = 32 shows the same shape on its smaller

what the runs say

  sampler agreement, L = 64, T = 2.300: metropolis <|m|> = 0.4724, cluster
  <|m|> = 0.4350; d = 2.00 with the 8000-step bootstrap errors. d <= 3, but the
  metropolis error is still block-length sensitive (0.0178 / 0.0198 / 0.0186
  sweeps at 2000 / 4000 / 8000), so the verdict is agreement provisional, as
  the sheet anticipates. The cluster error is stable over the same three block
  lengths (0.0018 / 0.0019 / 0.0018).

  cluster T_c: fitted peaks T_peak(32) = 2.3489 and T_peak(64) = 2.3121, so
  T_c = 2 T_peak(64) - T_peak(32) = 2.2752, +0.26% from 2.26919 (inside Part
  2's 2% gate), with a block-bootstrap error of 0.0006 at every block length of
  2000, 4000 and 8000 moves and 0 failed fits in 1500 replicates.

  work per independent sample, L = 64, T = 2.300: the cluster chain has
  tau_moves = 4.476 and <c> = 935.6 spins per move over the recorded rows (the
  stdout figure of 929.3 also averages the discarded moves), so Equation 17
  gives tau_work = 1.022 sweeps against the metropolis 607.7 sweeps: 594 times
  less spin-update work per independent sample at that temperature (and 1236
  times at T = 2.35, the largest ratio on the window grid).
