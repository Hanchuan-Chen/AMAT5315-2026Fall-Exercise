## Task 3: RED test for the correlation analysis (commit)

**Files:** create `week3/scripts/test_part3.py`.

- [ ] Write a stdlib `unittest` suite that fails against a missing `errors.py`/`chi_bootstrap.py` and, once they exist, pins the contract:
  - `naive_stderr` is `std(ddof=1)/sqrt(n)` and `block_stderr` with 50 blocks is the SEM of 50 equal block means, on a hand-checked fixture;
  - `autocorrelation` returns `rho(0) = 1` exactly, is symmetric under the direct `sum_k x_k x_{k+t} / (n v)` sum to `1e-12`, and equals it over the first ten lags for a non-trivial series;
  - `tau_int_from_rho` reproduces the checker's loop: `0.5` for an all-negative tail, `0.5 + phi/(1-phi)` (to the truncation) for `rho(t) = phi^t`, and the hand-computed `0.5 + 4 * 0.02` for a constant positive tail that trips the six-times rule at `t = 4`;
  - the truncation stops at the first negative `rho` even when the six-times rule has not fired;
  - `tau_int` of a synthetic AR(1) series with `phi = 0.9` and 20000 samples is within a few percent of `(1 + phi) / (2 (1 - phi)) = 9.5`;
  - the merged grid prefers the window rows inside `[2.0, 2.6]` and the coarse rows outside, and yields 27 temperatures;
  - `bootstrap_replicate` on a synthetic two-size window whose `chi` is an exact parabola returns the exact vertex and `T_c = 2 T_peak(64) - T_peak(32)`;
  - a flat or upward-curving susceptibility is counted as a failed fit, not as a `T_c`.
- [ ] Run `.venv/bin/python -m unittest scripts.test_part3 -v` inside `week3/` and record the import/attribute failures.
- [ ] Commit `test: specify the week 3 part 3 uncertainty analysis`.
