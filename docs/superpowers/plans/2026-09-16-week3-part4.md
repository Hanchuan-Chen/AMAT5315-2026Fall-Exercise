**Goal:** Produce Part 4 of the week-3 sheet — the Wolff single-cluster update
behind the existing `--update wolff` flag, the two critical-window cluster
runs, the sampler-agreement chart with its provisional-agreement verdict, the

reader and Equation 13, `peaks.py` owns Equation 10 and the five-point fit,
`chi_bootstrap.py` owns the block bootstrap. `magnetization_compare.py` and
`compare.py` only add the two Part 4 charts.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.98.1 (`cargo`, `rand 0.9`, `rand_chacha 0.9`), Python
3.12 from `week3/.venv` (`numpy`, `matplotlib`), stdlib `unittest`, the

**Files:** create `week3/tests/wolff.rs`, `week3/scripts/test_part4.py`; modify `week3/tests/contract.rs`, `week3/tests/reproducibility.rs`.

- [ ] `tests/wolff.rs`:
  - `add_probability` is `1 - exp(-2/T)` at `T = 2.0, 2.3, 2.6, 3.0` and `T_c`; monotone decreasing in `T`; goes to 1 at small `T` and 0 at large `T`;
  - `bond_added` compares a supplied uniform against the probability, and 200000 draws of a hot aligned bond accept at the predicted rate;
  - a checkerboard has no aligned neighbour, so `grow_cluster` returns exactly the seed whatever the draws and whatever the temperature;
  - an all-up lattice at `T -> 0` grows the whole `L^2`; a one-down lattice at `T -> 0` grows exactly the 15 up sites from an up seed and exactly the one down site from the down seed;
  - `grow_cluster` never holds a site whose spin differs from the seed's;
  - `cluster_flip` flips exactly the grown set: the changed sites equal `grow_cluster` from the same lattice and stream (the seed is the move's first draw), the set is connected, and the size matches;
  - every site in the cluster changes spin and no site outside it does, at several temperatures (no acceptance test);
