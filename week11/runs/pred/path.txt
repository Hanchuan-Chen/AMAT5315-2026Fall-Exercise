Path (2 steps): Factoring → CircuitSAT → SpinGlass/SimpleGraph/i32

  Step 1: Factoring → CircuitSAT
    num_variables = O(num_bits_first * num_bits_second)
    num_assignments = O(num_bits_first * num_bits_second)

  Step 2: CircuitSAT → SpinGlass/SimpleGraph/i32
    num_spins = O(num_assignments * num_variables)
    num_interactions = O(num_assignments * num_variables)

  Overall:
    num_spins = O(num_bits_first^2 * num_bits_second^2)
    num_interactions = O(num_bits_first^2 * num_bits_second^2)
